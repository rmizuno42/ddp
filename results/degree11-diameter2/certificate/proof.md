# Order, regularity, diameter and symmetry

Use the labels and shifts of the [construction](../README.md#construction). The 17 fixed vertices and thirteen disjoint seven-point fibres give $17+13\cdot7=108$ vertices. Each matching joins distinct fibres. The four shifts within an outer pair are distinct, so they give four disjoint matchings. The fixed-edge list uses each pair once. These properties give an undirected simple graph.

## Degrees

- Every fixed vertex has four neighbours in the fixed subgraph and all seven vertices of one centre fibre.
- A vertex of $C_0,C_1$ or $C_2$ has three fixed neighbours, two centre neighbours and six outer neighbours. A vertex of $C_3,C_4,C_5$ or $C_6$ has two fixed neighbours, three centre neighbours and six outer neighbours.
- An outer vertex has one neighbour in each of the seven centres and four neighbours in its paired outer fibre.

Every degree is 11 and the size is $108\cdot11/2=594$. The five edge counts are 34 within the fixed subgraph, 119 from fixed vertices to centres, 63 between centres, 294 from centres to outer vertices and 84 within the three outer pairs.

## Diameter certificate

For each unordered nonadjacent pair $u,v$, intersect their two neighbour sets in the edge list generated by the construction. Grouping the pairs by intersection size gives the following finite certificate. [Executable calculation](symmetry.py) · [Saved counts](orbits.json)

| Common neighbours | 1 | 2 | 3 | 7 | 9 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Nonadjacent pairs | 4,854 | 255 | 63 | 9 | 3 |

The entries total 5,184, exactly $\binom{108}{2}-594$. Every nonedge thus has a two-step path. For example, vertices 0 and 5 are nonadjacent and have the path $0,1,5$. The diameter is exactly 2. Independent full-distance computations give 108 ordered pairs at distance 0, 1,188 at distance 1 and 10,368 at distance 2. ## Identification of the group

Let $T$ translate all fibre coordinates by 1 while fixing vertices 0–16. Define $M$ to fix those 17 vertices, send $C_i(x)$ to $C_i(-2x)$ and send $A_r(x)$ to $B_{r+1}(-2x+s_{r+1})$ and $B_r(x)$ to $A_{r+1}(-2x+s_{r+1})$. The subscript is taken modulo 3. The shift identities $a_i+b_i=1$ and $2\{0,3,5,6\}=\{0,3,5,6\}$ in $\mathbb F_7$ show that $M$ preserves the construction. On all 108 labels one has $T^7=M^6=1$ and $MTM^{-1}=T^5$. Since 5 has order 6 modulo 7, these permutations generate the affine group of order 42.

On the uncoloured graph, nauty 2.9.3 returns order 252 and five generators. Closing the five permutations gives exactly 252 distinct automorphisms. Their centre is cyclic of order 6 and meets the affine subgroup only in the identity. A generator of the centre together with $T,M$ generates all 252 elements. Thus the full group is C₆ × AGL(1,7). The [raw nauty output](automorphisms.json) and [derived permutations and orbits](orbits.json) use the same vertex IDs as the edge list.

In the published labels, the saved central generator acts on the 17 fixed-subgraph vertices as

$$
(1\;2\;3)(5\;6\;7)(8\;11\;14)(9\;10\;15\;16\;12\;13).
$$

It permutes the centre fibres by $(0\;1\;2)(3\;4\;5)$ while preserving $x$. On both members of outer pair $r$ it sends $r$ to $r-1$ modulo 3 and adds $5,3,6$ to $x$ for $r=0,1,2$, respectively. Its order-six action is visible already on the six labelled vertices of the last cycle.
